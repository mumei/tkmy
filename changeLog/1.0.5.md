# TKMY 1.0.5

## English

### Cost estimates for new models

- Added pricing for GPT-6 Astra and more Claude models, including Fable 5.1, Mythos 5.1, Opus 5, Sonnet 5, and Haiku 4.5.
- Updated GPT-5.6 Sol to the current promotional rates and added support for the `gpt-5.6` alias. Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1 use their reduced cache-read rate.

Pricing was reviewed on September 6, 2026. Estimates use standard rates, with short-context rates for OpenAI models; service-tier and long-context surcharges are excluded. Historical usage without a logged cost is recalculated with the updated catalog.
